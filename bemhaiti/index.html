What we do

Our staff in the U.S. and Haiti continue to serve the people of Haiti by spreading the gospel of Jesus Christ, feeding the hungry, and providing education for the youth.

  • Mercy Hospital

    Clinical area opening soon

    Health care is so limited in southern Haiti. We are building Mercy Hospital in Les Cayes to serve our neighbors with competence and compassion. Each step is bathed in prayer.

    See the progress

  • Pastor education

    Strong churches need well-taught pastors. We provide education to Haitian pastors through bi-weekly Pastor education, the annual Pastors Conference and the Grand Convention.

  • Food and emergency relief

    When the 2021 earthquake struck, our team distributed rice to families in and around Les Cayes. We also bring formula for babies at the general hospital in Les Cayes. Through it all, we share food, emergency supplies and, most importantly, the love and hope of Jesus Christ.

  • Church Partnership Program

    Haitian pastors are praying daily to be in partnership with someone for the advancement of the kingdom of God. You happen to be their answer. Our Church Partnership Program is a church-covenant, kingdom-minded partnership that puts a face on missions and joins two congregations in prayer for each other.

    Learn about church partnership

  • Sponsor a child

    Millions of Haiti’s children know extreme poverty all too well. At the RFA Homes in Chantal and Ducis, a monthly sponsorship gives a child health care, school materials, nutritious food and more.

    Sponsor a child with RFA Haiti

  • Christmas in Haiti

    Every Christmas we serve meals to Haitian children, share the Christmas story and give out toys. Share joy with a child this Christmas.

    Give for Christmas

Group mission trips to Haiti are temporarily postponed because of the dangerous conditions on the ground. Our staff in Haiti carry the work forward.

Mercy Hospital in Les Cayes

In southern Haiti, good health care is hard to find. Mercy Hospital is our answer: a place to care for the people of the region with the highest level of competency and compassion. Local crews and visiting volunteers have raised it step by step, by faith.

June 2025 update: “We will soon be opening the clinical area of our Mercy Hospital.” Thank you for your support, and please keep praying for the people of Haiti and for this important project.

“This project is God’s project, not ours.”

Pastor Lubin Beaucejour, April 2025

About BEM

Who we are

Bethesda Evangelical Mission (BEM) is a Christian non-profit established in 1984, based in Cheshire, Connecticut and Les Cayes, Haiti. Our focus is empowering Haitians through education and opportunities to learn life skills, and promoting better health through medical clinics in some of the most neglected areas of southern Haiti.

Our story

BEM began in Les Cayes with prayer meetings in a family living room. On January 1, 1984, the group became a church and held its first Sunday worship. Longing to see deliverance, healing and restoration, they turned to John 5 and named it Bethesda Evangelical Church of Les Cayes. Mission trips to nearby villages soon planted new churches.

Today our updates from the field are written by Pastor Lubin Beaucejour, a son of Les Cayes. In 2022, BEM also formed a partnership with the town of Les Cayes.

Why we do it

“We serve Christ by answering His call to bring His glorious gospel wherever we go and by being His hands and feet - giving, serving, listening, healing, helping, training.”

Our long-term goal is to empower and cultivate resilient Haitians to be the answer to their own country’s many needs.
