What we do

Electrical and generator services in Tolland, CT

Electrical work remains the core of AJP Electric, from a single outlet to a whole-home generator, for homes across Tolland and Hartford Counties.

  • Whole House Generators

    Outages can shut off heat, refrigeration and sump pumps. As a Generac dealer, AJP Electric sizes and installs standby generators with automatic transfer switches, then tests the system and walks you through it.

  • Electrical Panel Upgrades

    Breakers that keep tripping, an old fuse box, or a generator or EV charger on the way? AJP Electric upgrades outdated or undersized panels so your home can handle modern loads.

  • Home EV Charger Installation

    A standard outlet charges slowly. AJP Electric checks your panel capacity, runs a dedicated circuit and installs a Level 2 charger in the garage or driveway, tested and ready for daily use.

  • Lighting Installation

    Dim rooms and too few switches? AJP Electric installs new fixtures, dimmers and multi-location switches, plus entry, garage and outdoor lighting for safety and curb appeal.

  • Outlets & Wiring

    Too few outlets, cracked or warm receptacles, or no GFCI protection in the kitchen, bath or garage? AJP Electric adds outlets, replaces worn ones and upgrades protection where it’s needed.

  • More Electrical Work

    No job too small: recessed lighting, ceiling fans, kitchen and bath wiring, home office circuits, pool and hot tub wiring, electrical inspections and generator maintenance.

About

A Tolland electrical contractor since 2014

AJP Electric is a locally operated electrical contractor based in Tolland, Connecticut, founded in 2014. The crew serves homeowners across Tolland and Hartford Counties with generator installation, panel upgrades, lighting, EV charging and everyday residential electrical work.

The business grew out of a simple observation: many homeowners struggled to find a reliable electrician for smaller projects around the house. That gap still shapes how AJP Electric works: no job too small, straightforward recommendations and quality workmanship from a local team.

Expect clear communication, practical guidance instead of pressure tactics, and an estimate before any work begins.
