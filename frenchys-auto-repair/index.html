Services

Auto repair services in Wallingford

The jobs on our sign, from a warning light on the dash to a panel that needs paint.

  • Computer Diagnostics

    A warning light is on and you don’t know why. We read your car’s computer to find the fault, then tell you what we found.

  • Full Mechanical

    Brakes, suspension, engine, transmission and electrical work. Whatever is grinding, slipping or leaking, call and tell us what you’re noticing.

  • Collision & Autobody

    Dents, scrapes and crash damage. We repair and paint panels in-house, so your car comes back looking like it should.

  • Restoration Services

    Got an older car or truck worth saving? Restoration is on our sign, and no job is too big: we’ve swapped the frame under a Ford F-150. Call to talk it through.

  • CT Emissions Testing

    Emissions test due? Stop in. Appointments aren’t necessary, but you can ask for one.

  • Wheel Alignment

    Car pulling to one side or wearing tires unevenly? Our alignments come with a printout of the readings before and after the service.

  • A/C Service

    Air blowing warm? We service A/C systems, including newer cars that use R-1234yf refrigerant.

  • Pre-Purchase Inspections

    Buying a used car? Have it looked at before you pay for it.

About

A family shop on Main Street in Wallingford

Frenchy’s Auto Repair describes itself as a “family owned and operated business since 1976.” You’ll find us at 508 Main Street in Wallingford, under the blue-and-white sign.

One shop handles a lot: computer diagnostics, mechanical repair, collision and paint work, restoration and Connecticut emissions testing. That means fewer stops when your car needs more than one thing.

In our own words, “Our main goal is to make car repair painless and easy as possible.”
