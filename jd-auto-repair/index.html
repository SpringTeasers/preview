About the shop

About JD Auto Repair

JD Auto Repair LLC is an automotive repair shop at 158 Montauk Ave in downtown New London, Connecticut. It is an active Connecticut LLC and holds Connecticut DMV licence U9191.

The work the shop shows is hands-on: engine work, starter replacement, cars up on the lift in its own bay. You see the same bay in the photos above.

The shop posts in both English and Spanish — "Motor de arranque / Starter", "Día de motor!" — and it shows up locally: in July 2023 it ran a booth at New London Sailfest. Bring your questions in either language.
