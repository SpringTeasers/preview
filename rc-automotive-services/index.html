About the shop

About R-C Automotive Service

R-C Automotive Service is an auto repair shop at 213 Colony St in Meriden, between Camp Street and Columbia Street. It does complete repairs on foreign and domestic vehicles, specializes in on-board and electrical diagnosis, and sells and services tires.

The shop has been on Facebook since 2015. In 2016 it kept an airbag-recall notice posted outside the shop and called the dealer for its customers to set up the recall work.

Customers' reviews mention fair prices and being told the most reasonable way to fix something. To ask a question or book a repair, call (203) 237-3632, Monday to Friday.
